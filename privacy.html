Pulse Privacy

Pulse is a personal health and training dashboard. It reads health and fitness data you authorize through the Google Health API and lets you log strength-training data on your device.

In short: Google Health access is read-only. Pulse does not sell your health or training data, use it for advertising, or expose Google access tokens to browser JavaScript.

Google Health data accessed

Depending on what Google Health makes available for your account and Fitbit device, Pulse may read activity, exercise, heart-rate telemetry, HRV, resting heart rate and sleep data. Heart-rate telemetry is requested only when you open a logged workout detail to add Fitbit context to that session.

Strength-training logs

Exercises, sets, reps, weight, RPE, routines, goals and workout history are stored in this browser's local storage in this version. They are not uploaded to the Pulse server. Clearing browser/site data can delete these logs, so use the training-log export in Settings if you want a backup.

How authentication is stored

After you grant access, the server stores the Google refresh token in an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie. Browser-side JavaScript never receives that refresh token. Health API responses are marked not to be cached by the server.

Storage and sharing

This prototype does not use a server database for your Google Health history or strength logs. Dashboard data is requested when you sync. Pulse does not sell or share health data with advertisers or data brokers.

Calculated scores and suggestions

Pulse Recovery, Pulse Sleep Score, Pulse Load, estimated 1RM, progression suggestions and recovery-performance correlations are app-generated wellness and training indicators. They are not Fitbit proprietary scores, medical diagnoses, or guarantees of performance.

Disconnecting

You can clear the Pulse Google session by disconnecting in the app or clearing site data. You can also revoke Pulse from your Google Account permissions. Local strength logs remain on the device until you clear site data or delete them in a future version.

Scope

This page describes the personal prototype included with this deployment. If Pulse is later offered publicly, its privacy policy, storage model, retention controls and Google verification configuration should be reviewed before launch.
